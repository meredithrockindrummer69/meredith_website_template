I'm Meredith, a Chicago-based electric and upright bassist.
I also play cello and synth, and have a band of my own called Shoulderbird.
I have a jazz bass degree from UW Madison under Nick Moran and Richard Davis and did a year of graduate study with Larry Gray at University of Illinois.
I've played and toured with:

  • Tobacco City, G'nite, Bendigo Fletcher, The Julia Blair Band, Thelma Plum, Hannah Frances, Astrachan, Smushie, Ava Brenan, Minor Moon, Hemlock, Elijah Berlow, Rich Robbins, Jackie Hayes, Morpho
